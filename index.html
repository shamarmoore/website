Work

Hurricanes and Jamaica

175 years of storms, 1851 to 2025. I rebuilt NOAA’s best-track archive around Jamaica’s coastline, joined it to damage figures from the PIOJ, ECLAC and the World Bank, and asked plain questions: how often storms come, which way they come from, and why some years hurt far more than others. Hurricane Melissa in October 2025 started it.

Python for the pipeline, statistics and an early-warning model; Power BI for the report.

Read the study on GitHub Open the Power BI project

I’m not a meteorologist. I work with data, and I followed the numbers even where they contradicted what I expected.
